# COMRDY — transaction workbench

## Locked direction

Designing a browser workbench for engineers testing AI checkout. The primary job is to introduce a known failure and inspect the contract, observed merchant state, and payment decision. Direct implementation is authorized; no speculative brand exploration or provider claims.

Primary reference: Refero's Vercel style, a bright schematic system with product precision, fine rules, restrained surfaces, and a concrete technical graphic. Preserve the white canvas, strong ink, fine grid, compact navigation and product-first composition. Keep COMRDY's Geist and Signal Blue identity.

Borrow only: 19–86's ruled information rows instead of card grids; Linear Changelog's weight-500 type and tight information hierarchy. Linear's dark palette, frosted glass and pill navigation are deliberately outside the lock. The Linear detail screen (https://refero.design/pages/1a493a2c-7d09-4143-b0dd-745781ea7991) informs the central evidence view and quieter metadata. Reference catalog:

- [Vercel](https://vercel.com) — Refero style `32824f01-0f25-473e-b57f-d8f2121bbcb1`: bright canvas, schematic product emphasis, restrained surfaces.
- [19–86](https://19-86.fr) — Refero style `7a8c99db-5ce7-4fa4-b491-8f1fcac18991`: fine ruled rows; borrow the information rhythm only.
- [Linear Changelog](https://linear.app/changelog) — Refero style `11d3e58a-87d7-4a9a-bbf5-720f4fd3ffc6`: weight-500 type and compact hierarchy; omit its dark palette.
- [Linear detail screen](https://refero.design/pages/1a493a2c-7d09-4143-b0dd-745781ea7991): viewed screenshot; central content, secondary metadata and fine dividers.

The coordinator retrieved the three complete style references before implementation. This concise record preserves the source identities and bounded borrowing; the raw research dump is not a runtime or handoff dependency.

## Expanded visual direction — user-approved refinement

The user explicitly requested more life, color, a recognizable icon, professional SVG motion, a complete shared-shell treatment and an Open Graph image. Preserve the researched structure while expanding the visual identity: Signal Blue becomes the technical demonstration canvas; pale cyan distinguishes the confirmed input; ink anchors the footer and payment boundary. Color communicates the input/check/output stages rather than decorating unrelated containers.

The signature mark is an open C with a separate gate and a passing cyan signal. It appears in the navigation, workspace, footer, browser icon and social image. A custom SVG trace illustrates merchant drift and the closed capture boundary; its short entrance runs once and can be replayed explicitly. Reduced motion shows the complete final diagram without movement. The schematic stays explicitly illustrative.

The homepage keeps the white left-aligned proposition, now balanced by a blue instrument panel, distinct numbered sections and an ink payment boundary. The app gains a visible three-step setup indicator driven by its existing contract/confirmation state, restrained tinted context and stronger surface separation. No invented metrics or provider results. The same mark, tokens and diagram compose the 1200×630 social asset.

Social assets: original editable SVG at `public/brand/comrdy-og.svg`, rendered PNG at `public/brand/comrdy-og.png` (1200×630), vector mark at `public/brand/comrdy-mark.svg`, file-based SVG browser icon and 180px Apple icon in `src/app`. The PNG was rendered with the existing Sharp dependency; no installation or image-generation service was used. Metadata uses APP_URL, then VERCEL_PROJECT_PRODUCTION_URL, then local development origin. The social image carries the same explicit illustrative/simulation scope as the page.

## Hero concept replacement — latest user direction

The user rejected the size-comparison hero. Replace that concept completely on the homepage and social image with an engineering test bench: confirmed contract → controlled shopper → verification gate → inspectable evidence. The visual is a continuous signal rail with four labelled stages, a branching control path and a clear evidence output, not a product/price comparison or a card grid. Keep the logo, white/ink/blue/cyan palette, researched typography and page structure. The real variant-fault scenario remains inside the lab and recorded evidence.

Every label describes the workflow rather than claiming a completed provider run. The bench is explicitly illustrative. Its finite SVG signal trace can be replayed; reduced-motion and no-JavaScript states retain the complete readable workflow. Hero copy, diagram accessibility text and OG artwork must contain no size-comparison concept.
